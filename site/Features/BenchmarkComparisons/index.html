How evidence is made

Evidence before conclusions.

Read the benchmark methodology ↗
01

Shared inputs

A seeded corpus fixes document bytes, vectors, graph edges and request schedules. Workload conditions and corpus hash travel with each report.

02

Real command paths

Clients call real services. Setup, warmup and correctness checks are outside the timer; queue cycles include enqueue, receive and ACK.

03

Correctness first

Writes are read back, graph results match a reference traversal, and queue completion rejects missing, duplicate or wrong-payload messages.

Qualification status

Early development, with limits visible.

Cluster qualification is in progress. Generator CPU, allocation and RSS describe the load generator and its sampling history, not database resource budgets. Endurance, overload and power-loss behavior remain unqualified.
